# How to play PALACIO

Two politicians duel for power. Win by bringing your rival's **Mandate** to 0,
or by leading the count on **Election Night**.

## Setup

- Each player starts with **20 Mandate** and a shuffled deck.
  - The World Edition deck has 40 cards: two of each common, one of everything
    else, and 2 random Leaders.
  - Edición Colombia uses its own 34-card deck.
- Each player draws a hand of **5**. The first player doesn't draw on their
  first turn.
- A player who has to draw from an empty deck **loses**.

## The field

Each player has:

- **5 Actor zones** (front row) for the politicians.
- **5 Backroom zones** (back row) for Set Policies, Equips and Set Scandals.
- A **deck** and **the Embassy** (La Embajada in Edición Colombia). The Embassy
  is the discard pile: destroyed, tributed and spent cards are "sent off as
  ambassador". Both Embassies are public, so either player can look inside.

## A turn

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Agenda** | You draw a card. "Each turn" abilities of your face-up Actors happen. |
| **Campaign 1** | Deploy, play Policies, Set a Scandal, change stances. |
| **Confrontation** | Attack with your Actors in Campaign stance. |
| **Campaign 2** | The same options as Campaign 1, after the fighting. |
| **Recess** | The turn ends. |

Per turn you get **one Actor deploy** and **one Scandal Set**, shared across
both Campaign phases. Policies have no per-turn limit (only free zones).

## Actors

Actors have **ATK** (attack) and **DEF** (defense) and come in three tiers:

- **Grassroots:** deploy freely.
- **Establishment:** cost a **tribute**. One of your Actors goes to the Embassy,
  and the new one takes its zone.
- **Leaders** (World Edition): also cost a tribute. Each has a unique power, and
  **only one Leader can be in office**; a new Leader must replace the old one.

Every Actor is in one of two **stances**:

- **Campaign:** face-up and upright. It can attack, and its ATK counts as votes
  on Election Night.
- **Resistance:** sideways, a defensive posture. Face-up or **face-down**
  (hidden from your opponent). It can't attack.

You can **change stance** once per Actor per turn. Not on the turn the Actor was
deployed, and not after it attacked. A face-down Actor flips face-up into
Campaign.

Abilities:

- **On deploy:** happens when the Actor is deployed face-up.
- **On flip:** happens when it's turned face-up.
- **Each turn:** at the start of each of your turns, while it's face-up.
- **When it falls:** when it goes to the Embassy, for any reason.
- **Passive** powers (e.g. "Your other Actors get +1 ATK") work only while the
  Actor is face-up.

## Battle (Confrontation phase)

A Campaign Actor that wasn't deployed this turn can attack once.

- **Against a Campaign Actor:** the higher ATK wins. The loser goes to the
  Embassy, and if the defender's Actor lost, the defender loses Mandate equal
  to the difference. A tie destroys both, with no damage.
- **Against a Resistance Actor:** the attacker's ATK against the defender's
  DEF. The lower one is destroyed; a tie destroys neither. **No Mandate damage
  gets through Resistance.** A face-down defender is flipped face-up.
- **Direct attack:** only when the opponent has no Actors at all. The full ATK
  becomes Mandate damage.

Reaching 0 Mandate ends the duel on the spot.

## Policies

- **Normal Policies** resolve once, then go to the Embassy. Some target an
  Actor (yours or your opponent's, depending on the card).
- **Equips** stay in a Backroom zone, attached to an Actor, usually yours
  (e.g. +1 ATK / +1 DEF). Hostile equips go on an opposing Actor. When that
  Actor leaves the field, its equips go to the Embassy with it.
- A Policy can be **Set face-down** and activated on a later Campaign phase.

## Scandals

Scandals are **Set face-down** (one per turn) and fire **automatically** when
their trigger happens: an attack, a direct attack, a big deploy, a Policy, an
Actor falling in battle, or Election Night. Then they go to the Embassy.

A Scandal never fires if its own cost would knock out its controller.

## The Embassy is not the end

Many cards bring cards back from an Embassy. **You choose which card.**

- Return to your hand: e.g. Presidential Pardon, the Ghostwriter.
- Revive straight onto the field, face-up in Resistance: e.g. Political
  Comeback, the Comeback Kid.
- Take from your opponent's Embassy: the Defector, Declassified Files.

A retrieval Policy with nothing it could bring back can't be played.

## Election Night

When **turn 12** ends (6 turns each), the votes are counted:

> **Votes = Mandate + ATK of your Campaign Actors + bonus votes**

Bonus votes come from cards like the Pollster, Bot Farm, Recount and the
Referendum Czar.

- A lead of **more than 3** wins the election.
- Anything closer goes to a **Runoff**: one more turn each with battle damage
  **doubled**, then a final count where any lead wins.
- A dead heat in the Runoff goes to whoever has **fewer cards in their
  Embassy** (the cleaner record), then to the player who went second.

The live poll in the middle of the board always shows the votes as they stand
now. Near the end you'll have to choose between campaigning in the open (more
votes, but exposed) and protecting yourself in Resistance.

## Controls

- **Tap a card** in your hand to see how you can play it, or **drag** it onto a
  zone.
- **Tap your Actor** in Confrontation, then tap its target, to attack (or drag
  it onto the target).
- **Long-press** (or right-click) any card to read it in full.
- **Esc** cancels a menu, a target choice or a drag.
