Privacy Policy

Duel for the World · Effective [DATE]

Duel for the World ("the game") is a card game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores on your device

The game remembers your settings (sound, music, vibration), your chosen difficulty and edition, and whether you have finished the tutorial. This is stored only on your own device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Playing against the computer

Matches against the computer run entirely on your device. No information is sent over the internet.

Playing online

When you play online, the game connects to our game server to send your moves to your opponent. You do not create an account and we do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Like any internet service, the server necessarily sees your device's IP address while you are connected; we use it only to run the match and to protect the service from abuse, and we do not keep it after the match or use it to identify you.

Permissions

  • Internet: for online matches.
  • Vibration: for the game's vibration effects (you can turn them off in Settings).

Advertising and analytics

The game currently shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change reaches the game, and it will explain what the advertising or analytics provider collects and how to opt out.

Children

The game is a political satire intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
