## App content (questionnaires)

- **Ads**: "No, my app does not contain ads" for now. Change it when ads arrive
  (and update the privacy policy).
- **Data safety**: "No data collected" and "No data shared". Settings stay on the
  device. Online play, once it's live, sends game moves only and has no accounts.
- **Target audience**: 13+ (or 16+). It is political satire, not a children's
  game. Don't select under-13 age groups.
- **Content rating (IARC questionnaire)**: no violence, sex, gambling, drugs or
  bad language. Answer "no" to real-money gambling; the game has no purchases.
  The rating comes out of your answers; satire with no violence or bad language
  usually lands in the lowest age bands.
- **News app**: No. **Government app**: No.

## Closed test (required for new personal developer accounts)

At least **12 testers** must stay opted in for **14 days in a row** before you
can apply for production access.

1. Play Console → Testing → Closed testing → create a track.
2. Add testers by email list (or a Google Group), upload the `.aab`
   (`pnpm android:bundle`) and roll out.
3. Share the opt-in link with testers. They must accept it and install from
   Google Play.
4. After 14 days, apply for production. Google asks what you learned from the
   test, so keep notes of tester feedback.
