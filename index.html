Privacy Policy

Duel for the World · Effective September 29, 2026

Duel for the World ("the game") is a card game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: we don't ask for your name, email or any account — the game shows ads through Google AdMob and sells one optional purchase through RevenueCat, both described below, and nothing else about you ever leaves your device.

What the game stores on your device

The game remembers your settings (sound, music, vibration), your chosen difficulty and edition, and whether you have finished the tutorial. This is stored only on your own device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Playing against the computer

Matches against the computer run entirely on your device. No information is sent over the internet.

Playing online

When you play online, the game connects to our game server to send your moves to your opponent. You do not create an account and we do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Like any internet service, the server necessarily sees your device's IP address while you are connected; we use it only to run the match and to protect the service from abuse, and we do not keep it after the match or use it to identify you.

Permissions

  • Internet: for online matches.
  • Vibration: for the game's vibration effects (you can turn them off in Settings).

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob between matches (never during one), and lets you choose to watch a rewarded ad in the Field Guide to earn in-game Donations. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your advertising ID and general technical information about your device (device type, approximate region from your IP address, app activity) and share it with its advertising partners. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before it ever requests an ad. You can reset or limit your device's advertising ID at any time (Android: Settings -> Google -> Ads), and manage Google's ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com. See Google's privacy policy for more: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Buying "Remove Ads" (or, in the future, a paid country edition) is handled by Google Play and by RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated purchase identifier and your purchase receipt, only to confirm and remember what you've bought — never your name, email or payment details, which stay with Google Play. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The game is a political satire intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
